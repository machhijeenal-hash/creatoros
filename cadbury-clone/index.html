Since 1824

200 years of Cadbury

From a grocery shop in Birmingham to the chocolate we know today — here’s how the “glass and a half” company came to be.

  1. 1824

    John Cadbury opens a shop

    A small grocer on Bull Street, Birmingham, selling tea, coffee and drinking chocolate.

  2. 1861

    The brothers take over

    Richard and George move the business to Bournville — a village built for its workers.

  3. 1905

    Dairy Milk is born

    George Cadbury Jr creates a bar with more milk than anyone thought possible.

  4. 1928

    A glass and a half

    The famous glass-and-a-half symbol appears on the Dairy Milk wrapper for the first time.

  5. 2024

    Two centuries of generosity

    Still made in Bournville, still celebrating the glass and a half in everyone.
